V. D. Satheesan

P. K. Kunhalikutty is a senior Indian politician from Kerala and a prominent leader of the Indian Union Muslim League (IUML). He has played a significant role in Kerala politics for several decades and is widely respected for his leadership, administrative experience, and dedication to public service. Throughout his political career, he has represented various constituencies and has actively contributed to the development of the state.

Kunhalikutty has held several important positions in government and public administration. He has been actively involved in promoting education, infrastructure development, economic growth, and social welfare programs. His efforts have focused on improving opportunities for citizens and supporting initiatives that contribute to the overall progress of Kerala.

Known for his experience, vision, and commitment to democratic values, P. K. Kunhalikutty continues to be an influential figure in Kerala's political landscape. His contributions to governance, policy-making, and community development have earned him recognition as one of the state's respected political leaders, dedicated to the welfare and advancement of the people.
