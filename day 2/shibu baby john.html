Shibu Baby John

Shibu Baby John is a prominent political leader from Kerala and a senior member of the Revolutionary Socialist Party (RSP). Born on 27 July 1963 in Kollam, Kerala, he is the son of the late Baby John, one of Kerala's most respected political leaders and a former minister. Shibu Baby John has played an important role in Kerala politics and has contributed significantly to public administration and social development. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

He completed his Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech.) degree from TKM College of Engineering, Kollam. After completing his education, he entered the business sector before actively participating in politics. His strong leadership qualities and commitment to public service helped him establish a successful political career. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Shibu Baby John began his legislative career by winning the Chavara Assembly constituency. Over the years, he has represented the constituency and earned recognition for his dedication to the welfare of the people. He has been actively involved in addressing issues related to employment, labor welfare, social justice, and regional development. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

From 2011 to 2016, he served as the Minister for Labour and Employment in the Government of Kerala. During his tenure, he introduced several initiatives aimed at improving workers' welfare, strengthening labor rights, and promoting employment opportunities across the state.
