Roji M. John

Roji M. John is a prominent political leader from Kerala and a member of the Indian National Congress (INC). He is known for his active involvement in student politics, youth leadership, and public service. Representing the Angamaly constituency in the Kerala Legislative Assembly, Roji M. John has emerged as one of the prominent young leaders in Kerala politics and has earned recognition for his dedication to public welfare and development.

Born on 20 May 1984 in Kerala, Roji M. John developed an interest in politics and social service at a young age. He pursued higher education with a strong academic background, earning a Master's degree (M.A.) and an M.Phil. degree. During his student years, he actively participated in student movements and leadership activities, which helped shape his political career and commitment to public service.

Roji M. John gained prominence through the National Students' Union of India (NSUI), the student wing of the Indian National Congress. He held several important positions within the organization and eventually became the National President of NSUI. His leadership skills, organizational abilities, and dedication to youth empowerment helped him gain recognition at both the state and national levels.
